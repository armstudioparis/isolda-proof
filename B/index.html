04

Before you sign

Florida condo safety law, 2022

A beautiful view does not pay a special assessment. Before you commit, I read what the brochure leaves out.

Reserve study
How much the building has saved for roofs, facades and structure.
Milestone inspection
Required for many Florida condos since 2022. Done, planned or overdue.
Board minutes
A year of minutes tells you what the brochure won’t.
Fees
What the monthly fee covers, per square foot, against the buildings next door.
Insurance
The policy, its renewal history, and what it costs the owners.
Flood zone
The map line that changes your insurance and your resale.
